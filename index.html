Common next steps

A day looks wrong

Open that date from Calendar and review the saved bleeding, sensation, and appearance.

02

03

Keep it marked not observed, or add the observation later only if you remember it.

04

Backup is not working

Confirm you are signed in with the same email on both devices, then reopen Settings.

05

You want to remove data

Settings has separate controls for device data, backed-up chart data, and account deletion.
