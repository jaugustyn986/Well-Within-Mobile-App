# Action 5 Source Register

This register separates external evidence from repository assertions. It is an issue-spotting and reviewer-preparation artifact, not a clinical or legal opinion.

## Source hierarchy

1. Controlling law, regulations, and regulator/platform guidance.
2. Official method-owner descriptions and authorized/licensed training materials.
3. Peer-reviewed primary research with population, training, and limitations visible.
4. Qualified clinical reviewer judgment documented against exact fixtures.
5. Repository specifications and automated tests.
6. Product or marketing interpretation.

Repository specifications and tests cannot substitute for levels 1–4.

## Method and clinical sources

| Source | What it supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Creighton Model — About](https://creightonmodel.com/about/) | The organization describes CrMS as a standardized system taught through professional services, individualized follow-up, and standardized teaching tools. | It does not validate Well Within's code mapping or automated interpretations. |
| [Creighton Model — Background](https://creightonmodel.com/background/) | Public description of mucus progression, Peak as the last clear/stretchy/lubricative day, long-cycle mucus patches, BIP/continuous-mucus complexity, and trained use. | It does not grant a license to reproduce textbook figures, sticker art, training language, or branded materials. |
| [Saint Paul VI Institute public chart/IP sample](https://saintpaulvi.com/PDF/CrMS_App_Copyright.pdf) | Public sample confirms several basic codes, the strongest-sign routine, Peak/P+3 concepts, mucus recording on light/very-light flow, and beginning-of-mucus-through-three-full-days-past-Peak among days of fertility for achieving pregnancy; it also lists additional bleeding/non-Peak rules, warns against learning without trained instruction, and identifies protected materials. | It is not a general software license, a complete clinical specification, or validation of Well Within's `Possible fertile pattern` implementation. |
| [FCCA — Creighton Model System](https://www.fertilitycare.org/creighton-model-system/) and [About FCCA](https://www.fertilitycare.org/about-fcca/) | Current organizational description of a standardized, practitioner-taught system and formal training/certification ecosystem. | It does not approve Well Within's implementation or affiliation. |
| [Comparison of woman-, expert-, and computer-picked Peak Day](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32101336/) | Peak identification can differ among user, expert, and algorithm; study population and agreement estimates are explicit. | It does not validate Well Within's algorithm or a universal patient-facing accuracy claim. |
| [Cervical mucus patterns and the fertile window](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8487651/) | Standardized daily observations in trained participants; Peak defined as last estrogenic-quality mucus; includes important non-Peak-only pattern nuance. | It does not establish that Well Within supports every pattern or can replace instruction. |
| [Randomized trial of Creighton instruction and time to pregnancy](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4861655/) | Participants received standardized instruction from a trained practitioner and used a standard diary. | It does not establish efficacy of an unassisted app implementation or support product outcome claims. |
| [Peak Day validation against a hormone monitor](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3893397/) | Describes trained-teacher instruction and limits around Peak Day use. | It does not validate Well Within's UI wording or automated cycle classifications. |
| [CDC — U.S. MEC Appendix F: Fertility Awareness-Based Methods](https://www.cdc.gov/contraception/hcp/usmec/fertility-awareness-based-methods.html) | Identifies contexts where FABM use becomes more complex or requires delay/special counseling, including postpartum, breastfeeding, perimenopause, irregular bleeding, discharge, and drugs affecting signs. | It does not define Well Within's method rules or approve an app interpretation. |

| --- | --- |

| [FDA classification PYT](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpcd/classification.cfm?id=PYT) | FDA currently lists Class II software that provides user-specific fertility timeframes for preventing pregnancy; an avoidance mode would be a material scope escalation. |

| [FTC — Premom fertility-app enforcement](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/05/ovulation-tracking-app-premom-will-be-barred-sharing-health-data-advertising-under-proposed-ftc) | Fertility-app health data and third-party disclosures receive direct enforcement attention. |
| [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) | Guideline 1.4.1 subjects medical/health accuracy and methodology claims to greater scrutiny; privacy and accurate metadata rules also apply. |
| [USPTO — Likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) | Trademark analysis centers on similar commercial impression, related goods/services, and potential source confusion. A clearance search is still required. |
| [FCCA affiliation instructions](https://www.fertilitycare.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/02-FCCA-Application-INSTRUCTIONS.pdf) | Current affiliation materials describe access to official materials/logos and make ownership/branding assertions relevant to clearance; counsel must verify exact rights and territories. |
| [U.S. Copyright Office — Copyright in General](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-general.html) | Copyright does not protect facts, ideas, systems, or methods of operation, but it can protect the particular text, illustrations, graphics, and other expression. |
| [U.S. Copyright Office — Circular 31](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ31.pdf) | Methods and systems are outside copyright, while the particular description, explanation, or illustration may be protected. |
| [Washington My Health My Data Act](https://app.leg.wa.gov/RCW/default.aspx?cite=19.373&full=true) | Illustrates state-level consumer-health obligations covering reproductive/sexual and algorithmically inferred health data; nationwide state-law review is required. |

## Required external inputs before approval

- The exact authorized source edition for each recording-code and instruction rule.
- Written permission/license status for any copied or adapted tables, prose, artwork, icons, or chart conventions.
- A qualified reviewer's signed decisions against the fixture pack.
- Counsel's trademark clearance and intended-use analysis for every jurisdiction of distribution.
- App Store privacy-label review against optional Supabase backup, Auth identifiers, feedback, and account linkage.
- A public privacy policy, an in-app link, retention/deletion disclosures, processor inventory, and incident-response/HBNR assessment.
