# Screenshot Storyboard

Status: **final release-candidate art direction; external upload remains pending**

## Story in one sentence

Well Within helps a user make a structured daily observation, see it reflected in a calm chart, review retrospective context across completed cycles, and export that record—while keeping an account and cloud backup optional.

## Final default order

### 1 — Daily action

**Headline**
One daily observation.
A chart that grows with you.

**Support line**
Each saved observation becomes part of your chart.

**Native proof**
Daily Entry on a synthetic mucus-observation day. Keep Sensation, Appearance, and the observation preview visible. The screen must show a real saved/editable state, not a fabricated mockup.

**Why first**
It explains the product in one glance and gives the viewer an achievable first action.

### 2 — Current chart

**Headline**
See your chart
take shape.

**Support line**
The calendar reflects the observations you record.

**Native proof**
Current Calendar with the post-Peak summary, Peak outline on Cycle Day 8, P+1–P+3, non-Peak markers, bleeding, and the chart limitation visible. Use `captures/02-calendar-native.png` as the current source reference.

**Safety note**
The app copy `This reflects your chart; it does not confirm ovulation` must remain legible.

### 3 — Cycle overview

**Headline**
Your cycle,
all in one view.

**Support line**
See its length, Peak marker, and recorded pattern together.

**Native proof**
Completed Cycle Detail showing Cycle Overview, the recorded mucus pattern, and the consolidated `P+3 recorded` milestone. The chosen cycle must have one internally consistent range and no unresolved cycle-start ambiguity.

**Safety note**
No `fertile window`, range prediction, ovulation confirmation, or fertility-status headline may be added outside the native UI.

### 4 — History over time

**Headline**

**Support line**

**Native proof**
Cycle History with three completed, eligible synthetic cycles and one current cycle. Keep First mucus sign, Peak marker, and Peak to next cycle visible. Every range must match the corresponding Cycle Detail screen.

**Safety note**
This is comparison of recorded chart context, not a forecast of the next cycle.

### 5 — Export

**Headline**
Your chart,
ready to export.

**Support line**
Create a PDF for your records or share it on your terms.

**Native proof**
Cycle Detail with Export visible plus a real generated PDF detail layered subtly over the device. Do not show a share recipient, email address, or personal data.

### 6 — Privacy choice

**Headline**
Your chart.
Your backup choice.

**Support line**
Use the app without an account. Add backup only if you choose.

**Native proof**

**Privacy reconciliation**
Privacy closes the story after the product value is established. The frame uses the reconciled local-by-default, optional-backup language in `PRIVACY_RECONCILIATION.md`. If the published policy or App Store answers change, update the native wording and recapture this frame.

## Art direction

- Master size: **1320×2868 portrait**, an accepted Apple 6.9-inch screenshot size.
- Layout: large, left-aligned two-line headline; one short support sentence; a large straight-on native app surface that can continue slightly below the frame.
- Type: SF Pro/system sans; warm charcoal `#3F3A36`; semibold headline; regular support copy.
- Background rotation: restrained blush, pale sage, warm cream, restrained blush, pale sage, restrained blush. Privacy intentionally closes on the same warm family that opens the story.
- Device treatment: subtle warm shadow and generous rounded mask. Do not add chrome that competes with the app.
- Preserve the native calendar color system, Peak outline, P+ labels, text labels, and non-color markers.
- No heavy gradients, stock botanicals, medical imagery, badges, seals, fertility icons, or decorative data visualizations.
- Marketing treatment may add only the background, headline, support sentence, device mask/shadow, and a genuine export preview. Do not redraw or rewrite native app UI.

## Frame-level review questions

For every frame, reviewers should be able to answer yes to all of these:

- Is the visible screen from the current release candidate?
- Does it use only the approved synthetic fixture?
- Can a viewer identify the product action or benefit in under three seconds?
- Are dates, Peak/P+ markers, and cycle ranges consistent across frames?
- Is every limitation needed to understand the claim still visible?
- Would a reasonable user read this as observation and retrospective context rather than prediction?
- Does meaning remain available without color alone?
- Is there any method-affiliation, clinical-outcome, privacy-absolute, or unsupported certainty claim?
