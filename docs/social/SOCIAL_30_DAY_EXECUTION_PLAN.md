# Well Within Social 30-Day Execution Plan

Last updated: 2026-07-04

## Goal

Restore social as a learning and acquisition system, not a daily content treadmill.

North star: activated social-acquired users per week.

While the account is still tiny, the temporary social scorecard is:

1. Qualified reach from Reels.
2. Profile visits.
3. Saves, shares, comments, DMs, and follows.
4. Profile quality signals: bio clicks, meaningful follows, profile visits, and direct replies.
5. Qualitative audience language captured from comments, DMs, interviews, and adjacent communities.

## Week 0: Reset and Freeze

Status: underway.

- Pull live Instagram metrics.
- Build the social dashboard.
- Freeze routine daily content packages.
- Create the hypothesis backlog.
- Add automation gates so future runs cannot draft content without evidence.
- Mark the two July 4 draft packages as not approved for publishing unless manually revived.

## Week 1: Audience-Language Sprint

Deliverable: enough audience language to make the next three tests feel native to the people we want, not just internally reasonable.

- Mine adjacent Instagram/TikTok accounts, app reviews, fertility-awareness educator content, TTC/community language, and privacy-first cycle app discourse.
- Capture exact phrases, objections, anxieties, desires, and hooks.
- Map each useful observation to one backlog hypothesis or create a new hypothesis with evidence level 1.
- Identify five practitioners, educators, creators, or community nodes for possible trust transfer.
- Keep App Store attribution as a parallel readiness task, but do not let it block this sprint.

Pass condition: at least ten exact audience-language observations and three sharper test hooks.

## Week 2: First Three Native Tests

Deliverable: three bounded Instagram tests, likely Reels first.

Test order:

1. H1: Human anti-prediction positioning.
2. H2: Charting confusion community prompt.
3. H3: One real daily entry demo.

Each test must have:

- One target metric.
- One decision rule.
- A freshness check.
- A claim-safety check.
- A manual review before publishing.

Pass condition: at least one test beats the current Reel baseline or generates a concrete audience response.

## Week 3: Referral And Trust Channel Probe

Deliverable: decide whether Instagram should be paired with practitioner/creator/community distribution.

- Reach out to or deeply research five qualified fertility-awareness educators, practitioners, creators, or communities.
- Capture what they would need to trust or recommend Well Within.
- Decide whether to create one practitioner-facing asset, creator brief, or collaboration test.
- Keep public Instagram content limited to what the first tests justify.

Pass condition: two qualified referral hypotheses or a clear decision to keep this channel on hold.

## Week 4: Decide Scale, Pivot, or Research

Deliverable: one decision memo.

- If a post earns intent signal: make 2-3 variants and consider a tiny paid test only if attribution is working.
- If reach improves but intent is still zero: keep testing hooks, not production polish.
- If nothing moves: pause posting and shift to referral, creator, and practitioner interviews.
- If app-funnel data is missing: do not run paid media.

## Parallel Readiness Track

App Store and app activation data are useful, but they are not the immediate bottleneck while the account has tiny reach. Set them up opportunistically:

- App Store Connect App Analytics access.
- Instagram-specific App Store campaign link or custom product page, if available.
- Privacy-safe activation proxy such as first observation recorded.
- No third-party fertility analytics SDK without explicit privacy review.

## Quality Bar

A post is not ready if it could have been written without seeing Well Within, the app metrics, or the user's actual problem.

The account needs fewer posts, better tests, and faster learning.
