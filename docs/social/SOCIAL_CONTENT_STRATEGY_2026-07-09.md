# Well Within Social Content Strategy

Date: 2026-07-09  
Status: recommended six-test validation system; no public posting is authorized by this document.

## Strategic choice

Primary audience for the next six tests:

> Method-aware or method-curious charting users frustrated by prediction-heavy, shallow, or cumbersome cycle apps.

This is the best current default because existing audience research repeatedly surfaces app friction, readable-chart needs, agency, privacy, and distrust of prediction. It is still a hypothesis and should be reconsidered after interviews and six tests.

Core promise:

> Record what you observe. See it in context. Keep control of your chart.

Proof points that can be shown now:

- Daily observation entry.
- Observation-based rules and explicit uncertainty.
- Calendar and multi-cycle history.
- Missing-day handling.
- Local-first storage and optional backup.
- Export and clear controls.

Do not claim without review:

- Ovulation confirmation.
- Pregnancy probability or improved conception chances.
- Contraceptive use or efficacy.
- Official affiliation or endorsement by a method organization.
- Universal `under 15 seconds` entry speed.
- Cloud deletion until an actual delete-everywhere control exists.

## Pillars and format roles

| Pillar | Role | Default format | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product in practice | Prove a real job | 8-20 second Reel | View profile or try the app |
| Observation literacy | Teach one idea in context | Reel; carousel only when swiping adds value | Save or share |
| Trust and boundaries | Reduce risk and clarify limits | Human/founder Reel with settings/UI cutaway | Read privacy/learn more |
| Listening | Capture language and objections | Story or specific human-led Reel | Reply or answer one question |
| Practitioner trust | Transfer credible human trust | Interview clip or co-created demo | Learn from the collaborator |

## Visual direction

Keep:

- Warm cream and restrained clay/green palette.
- Calm typography and whitespace.
- Real app UI.
- Honest, non-alarmist tone.

Change:

- Use real hands, phones, screens, and people.
- Make motion and proof visible in the first second.
- Use bolder on-screen hierarchy with fewer words.
- Treat the app’s cards, chart cells, and rose mark as the ownable graphic system.
- Use stock imagery only when it demonstrates a real routine; remove generic flowers, paper, and lifestyle filler.
- Stop exporting text-heavy document layouts as Reels.

## Hook system

Use one of five hook structures:

1. **Friction:** `Quick entry should not mean shallow data.`
2. **Boundary:** `A charting app should hold your observations—not override them.`
3. **Demonstration:** `Watch one entry become part of a chart.`
4. **Contrarian trust:** `This app says “not enough data” on purpose.`
5. **Identity:** `For people who want to read a chart, not receive a forecast.`

Avoid:

- `Charting can feel overwhelming.`
- `Start with one observation.`
- `Let the pattern build over time.`
- `Save this and follow for calmer charting notes.`

Those lines may be true, but they are exhausted on this account.

## Six-test sprint

Run over three weeks. Change one primary variable per test. Manual approval is required before publishing.

| Test | Hypothesis | Opening | Proof | Primary metric | Continue if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | H1 anti-prediction | `I built a chart that refuses to forecast you.` | Founder/human voice plus current summary | Qualified profile visits/follows | At least one intent signal or clear qualitative response |
| 2 | H3 product proof | `Watch one entry become part of a chart.` | Hand/phone entry -> save -> calendar | Watch completion and saves | Beats current 28-reach Reel or gets 2 intent signals |
| 3 | H4 privacy | `Where does your cycle data go?` | Local-first settings, optional backup, export | Saves/profile visits | Any privacy-specific response plus qualified reach |
| 4 | H2 listening | `What did your first charting app make harder?` | Human prompt plus three specific options | Useful replies/comments/DMs | At least one reusable audience phrase |
| 5 | H3 friction | `Quick entry should not mean shallow data.` | Show structured choices and resulting context | Completion/profile visit | Better completion than Test 2 or stronger intent |
| 6 | H5 trust transfer | `What would you need before recommending a charting app?` | Educator/practitioner answer or interview clip | Qualified conversation/referral | One follow-up conversation or permissioned collaborator |

Do not run Test 3 until current privacy claims and clear/delete semantics are reviewed. Do not imply endorsement in Test 6 without written permission.

## Profile and conversion system

Pinned posts after the six-test direction is approved:

1. Who Well Within is for.
2. What it does and does not do.
3. A real entry-to-chart demonstration.

Highlights after useful material exists:

- Start Here
- How It Works
- Privacy
- Export
- FAQ

Link path:

- Create an Instagram-specific App Store campaign link in App Store Connect.
- Verify the App Store screenshot set and first three frames before directing traffic.
- Match the Reel promise to the first screenshot and first onboarding promise.
- Measure product-page view, install, first intentional observation, second session, and D7 where privacy policy permits.

## Measurement sheet

For every test capture at 24h, 72h, and 7d:

- Reach.
- Non-follower reach.
- Three-second views.
- Average watch time and completion.
- Saves.
- Sends/shares.
- Comments/replies/DMs.
- Profile visits.
- Follows.
- Link taps.
- App Store product-page views/downloads when campaign data is available.
- Exact qualitative language.
- Decision: continue, vary, stop, or investigate.

At this account size, one specific DM or practitioner reply can be more informative than a small percentage change. Record both quantitative and qualitative evidence.

## Operating cadence

- Daily automation: bounded audience intelligence only; use Terra.
- Weekly automation: strategic hypothesis decision; use Sol.
- Content package: manually triggered only after a named test passes the pre-draft gate.
- Human owner: approves claims, footage, collaborators, and publishing.

After six tests:

- **Scale:** one idea earns repeated intent; make 2-3 variants.
- **Refine:** reach improves but intent does not; sharpen audience and payoff.
- **Pivot:** no format/message signal; move distribution effort to practitioner/creator interviews.
- **Pause:** App Store or product trust gaps make the promise unsafe to promote.
