## Strategic Rationale

The prior package was too close to recent "one observation" territory. This replacement keeps the account aligned with the research strategy while avoiding repeat fatigue: it moves from beginner-start reassurance into a trust-building privacy angle.

The source strategy says Well Within should act like a trusted fertility-awareness account first, then slowly lean into product fit. Privacy is a strong differentiator because fertility charting is personal and the product posture is specific: chart data stays on device unless backup is chosen. This gives the post a concrete reason to exist without using fear, prediction, diagnosis, or outcome claims.

## Freshness And Repeat-Prevention Check

- Recently published overlap reviewed: `Story — One Observation Is Enough To Start`, `Learning To Chart Starts With Noticing Not Knowing`, `5 Words That Make Fertility Charting Click`, and `What Fertility Charting Can And Cannot Tell You`.
- Repeat risk: low. This post does not reuse the "one observation," glossary, or can/cannot educational structures.
- Differentiator: privacy-first trust hook with quiet personal routine imagery.
- Avoid repeating: one-observation language, beginner "start today" reassurance, botanical glossary layouts, and product-demo screenshots.
