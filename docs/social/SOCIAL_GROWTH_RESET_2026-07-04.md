# Social Growth Reset: Well Within

- Date: 2026-07-04
- Owner stance: CPO / CMO / creative director
- Status: active strategic reset; pause routine content-package generation
- Primary objective: build a repeatable social growth system that earns attention, learns from evidence, and eventually drives qualified users to the app.

## Executive Decision

Stop treating Instagram as a daily content-output machine.

Until the measurement spine is repaired, the account should not generate new content packages unless the post passes the evidence gate below. The current loop has produced too many internally plausible posts and too little learning.

The new unit of progress is not "a post." It is a validated growth hypothesis.

## External Case-Study Takeaways

The transferable pattern is not "make nice educational carousels."

The repeatable growth loop from comparable and adjacent apps is:

1. Find a narrow emotional job-to-be-done.
2. Express it in platform-native creative.
3. Use creator, founder, expert, user, or community proof.
4. Test multiple hooks and formats.
5. Amplify winners with paid only after organic or qualitative evidence exists.
6. Measure downstream activation, not just impressions.

House of Marketers reports a Flo influencer campaign with 10.5M+ video views and 200K+ engagements, with creator videos amplified using Spark Ads. - Broad prediction language or privacy-risk behavior.

## Platform Reality

Instagram and TikTok are not neutral content calendars. They are recommendation systems.

Meta's Reels ranking documentation describes systems that collect candidate reels, evaluate signals like length and similarity, predict likely engagement or value, then rank. Suggested-account systems also use follow likelihood, mutual connections, and profile-visit likelihood. Current platform guidance and industry analysis consistently point toward original content, watch time/retention, sends/shares, saves, and profile interest.

Implication for Well Within:

- Static educational carousels may earn saves, but they are unlikely to solve reach alone.
- Reels, creator-style posts, founder voice, community prompts, and shareable identity hooks need real tests.
- We should optimize for "would someone send this to a friend?" and "would this person become an activated app user?", not "does this sound on-brand?"

Sources:

- https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-feed/
- https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/ig-suggested-accounts/

## New North Star

Primary north star:

Activated social-acquired users per week.

For Well Within, activation should mean:

- install or open from a tracked social source
- onboarding completed
- first daily observation recorded
- second session or second observation within 7 days

Upstream metrics remain useful, but only as diagnostics:

- reach and non-follower reach: platform distribution
- watch time / completion: attention
- saves and shares: usefulness or identity resonance
- profile visits and follows: trust/interest
- link taps and App Store product-page views: acquisition intent
- first observation and 7-day repeat: product fit

## No-Slop Gate

No content package may be generated unless it has all six:

1. One audience.
2. One painful or desirable user job.
3. One evidence source.
4. One hypothesis.
5. One target metric.
6. One decision rule after measurement.

Evidence levels:

- Level 0: internal hunch. Output allowed: research question only.
- Level 1: external pattern but no Well Within data. Output allowed: research, listening, or one low-cost test only if measurement exists.
- Level 2: external evidence plus weak internal signal. Output allowed: one organic test with a stop/scale rule.
- Level 3: repeated internal signal. Output allowed: controlled variants or small paid amplification if attribution exists.
- Level 4: app-funnel signal. Output allowed: scale, campaign, or paid learning budget.

Hard rule:

No evidence -> no content.
Weak evidence -> research/listening task or one bounded organic test.
Strong repeated evidence -> controlled variant.
Strong evidence plus app activation -> paid test or scale.

## Content Pillars To Test

Use these as hypotheses, not doctrine.

### 1. "This Explains Me" Body Literacy

Job: Help users recognize confusion without feeling broken.

Example angles:

- "Your chart is not supposed to make sense from one day."
- "The point is not to guess earlier. It is to notice more clearly."
- "Some cycle apps flatten your body into dates. Charting starts with signs."

Primary metrics: shares, saves, comments with self-identification.

### 2. Practical Charting Micro-Actions

Job: Make charting feel doable.

Example angles:

- "Before you interpret anything, write down what you noticed."
- "One daily habit that makes chart review less chaotic."
- "A note today is context later."

Primary metrics: saves, profile visits, first observation after install.

### 3. Trust And Boundaries

Job: Earn credibility by refusing overclaiming.

Example angles:

- "What a chart can show, and what it should not promise."
- "Private by design means: on device unless you choose backup."
- "No AI fertility claims. No prediction pressure."

Primary metrics: profile visits, follows, high-quality comments, low negative sentiment.

### 4. Human / Founder / Practitioner Voice

Job: Make the brand feel accountable and real.

Example angles:

- "Why I do not want Well Within to sound like a fertility fortune teller."
- "The charting mistake I see apps encourage."
- "What I would ask before trusting a cycle app."

Primary metrics: watch time, comments, follows, profile visits.

### 5. Product Proof

Job: Show the app only when the product moment is the proof.

Example angles:

- daily entry under 15 seconds
- chart history
- privacy behavior
- export/share for conversation or instruction

Primary metrics: profile visits, link taps, installs, first observation.

## Paid Strategy

Do not boost weak content.

Paid becomes useful only after:

- links are tracked with UTMs
- App Store/product-page views are visible
- install/open/onboarding/first-observation events are visible
- the creative has some organic or qualitative proof
- claims have been checked

Initial paid ladder:

1. $20-$50/day for 3-5 days on one proven post.
2. Same audience, 2-3 creative variants.
3. Kill if click quality or activation is weak.
4. Scale only when activated-user cost is plausible.

## Automation Redesign

Replace routine content generation with two active automations and one paused manual-only path:

### 1. Daily Instagram Audience Intelligence

Job: collect external user language, competitor comments, app review themes, and creator/educator patterns.

Output: 5-10 user-language insights, evidence level, and candidate hypothesis implications.

### 2. Weekly Instagram Growth Decision Review

Job: synthesize audience intelligence, metrics, and backlog evidence into a decision.

Output: one recommended next action: research/listening, one bounded hypothesis test, or pause/pivot.

### 3. Paused Content Package Path

Job: create a content package only if manually triggered and a hypothesis passes the no-slop gate.

Output: one test brief or one content package, never both by default.

## What I Am Retiring
